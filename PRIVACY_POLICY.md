# Privacy Policy for Stack Go

**Last Updated:** October 27, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 27, 2026  

Thank you for playing **Stack Go** ("the Game", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our mobile and desktop application.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Stack Go is designed to be a lightweight, arcade-style gaming experience. **We do not directly collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number).**

---

## 1. Information We Collect and Process

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
Stack Go stores gameplay progress and preference data locally on your device using local storage (`SharedPreferences`). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers:
- **Game Progress & Scores:** High score, best streak combo, total games played, total blocks stacked, and perfect placements count.
- **Game Settings:** Sound effects volume, background music volume, sound toggle state, music toggle state, haptic feedback preference, and reduced motion accessibility preference.
- **Unlocked Content:** Unlocked theme environments and selected active theme.

You can completely delete this data at any time by clearing the application's storage/cache in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

### B. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services
While we do not collect personal data directly, the Game integrates third-party services that may automatically collect certain diagnostic, device, and advertising information to facilitate ad delivery and service optimization.

Specifically, we use **Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)** to display advertisements (Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video ads). Google AdMob may collect and process:
- **Device Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (AAID on Android), IP address, device model, operating system version, and screen resolution.
- **Ad Interaction Data:** Views, impressions, clicks, interaction duration, and ad performance diagnostics.
- **Approximate Location Data:** General geographic location (country or city level derived from IP address) for regional ad serving.
- **Crash & Diagnostic Data:** Application crash logs and performance metrics to help detect and resolve technical issues.

For more details on how Google processes and safeguards data, please review:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google AdMob Privacy and Security](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

## 2. Permissions Used by the Application

Stack Go requests minimal device permissions necessary for core gameplay and monetization functionality:

| Permission | Android Name | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Vibration** | `android.permission.VIBRATE` | Provides tactile haptic feedback during block placement, combos, and game over (can be toggled off anytime in Settings). |
| **Internet Access** | `android.permission.INTERNET` | Required to request and display advertisements via the Google Mobile Ads SDK. |
| **Network State** | `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Allows the ad service to verify internet connectivity before attempting to load advertisements. |

We do not request sensitive permissions such as Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Precise Location (GPS), or External Storage access.

---

## 3. Advertising & Monetization

Stack Go is supported through advertising provided by Google AdMob:
- **Interstitial Ads:** Full-screen ads shown at natural transition points (e.g., following a game over or when navigating between major screens).
- **Rewarded Video Ads:** Optional ads that users voluntarily choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward (e.g., "Watch and Get a Chance" run revival).

### Opting Out of Personalized Advertising
You have the right to limit or opt out of personalized (interest-based) advertising at any time through your device settings:
- **Android:** Go to `Settings` > `Google` > `Ads` > `Delete advertising ID` or toggle `Opt out of Ads Personalization`.
- **iOS:** Go to `Settings` > `Privacy & Security` > `Tracking` > Disable tracking for Stack Go, or go to `Settings` > `Privacy & Security` > `Apple Advertising` > Turn off `Personalized Ads`.

When you opt out of personalized ads, you will still see advertisements in the game, but they will be contextual rather than tailored to your interests.

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA / CPRA)

Depending on your geographic location, you may have specific privacy rights regarding your data:

### European Economic Area (EEA) & UK Users (GDPR)
Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:
- Access the data processed about you by third parties.
- Request erasure or restriction of your data.
- Withdraw consent for personalized advertising at any time via your device settings or Google's ad settings.

### California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)
Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), you have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected by third parties.
- Request the deletion of your personal information.
- Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (as defined under California law) for cross-context behavioral advertising.

You can exercise these rights directly via your device's privacy controls or by contacting Google as specified in their privacy policy.
